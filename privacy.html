Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 22, 2026. This policy explains how BreadsBasket Studio handles information across our website, support channels, mobile software, and advertising-supported operations.

Scope and Controller

This Privacy Policy applies to BreadsBasket Studio websites, support pages, business contact routes, and mobile products that link to or reference this policy. BreadsBasket Studio acts as the controller of personal information processed through these services unless a third-party platform states otherwise under its own terms.

Information We Collect

We may collect information that you provide directly, such as your email address, support requests, screenshots, business communication details, and any contextual information you choose to send.

We may also collect technical information such as device type, operating system version, app version, language settings, crash traces, and service interaction details that help us understand product stability and usage patterns.

How We Use Information

BreadsBasket Studio uses information to respond to support requests, maintain and improve products, review product direction, investigate misuse, communicate with clients, and comply with legal or platform obligations.

We may also use information to understand feature adoption, operate support workflows, and keep product communication clear and accurate.

Product Diagnostics and Analytics

Some products may use diagnostics, analytics, or crash-reporting tools to identify service issues, understand product performance, and improve the reliability of releases over time. These systems may process technical events, aggregate behavior patterns, and error signals that help us keep products stable.

Advertising and Monetization

Some BreadsBasket Studio products may be offered as advertising-supported experiences. In those products, contextual information, device-level signals permitted by the platform, event completion markers, or limited campaign performance data may be processed to operate advertising and monetization responsibly.

Our goal is to keep ads tied to product sustainability, frequency control, and trustworthy user experience rather than uncontrolled clutter.

Data Sharing

We may share limited information with service providers that support hosting, diagnostics, analytics, advertising, fraud prevention, communications, or app distribution. We may also disclose information when required by law, legal process, or service protection needs.

We do not describe our ordinary operations as unrestricted resale of personal information. Any sharing is intended to support product delivery, compliance, safety, or service continuity.

Data Retention

We keep information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide support, maintain products, document communications, meet legal obligations, analyze service quality, and protect the integrity of our operations.

When information is no longer needed, we aim to delete, anonymize, or de-identify it where practical.

International Transfers

Because service providers, infrastructure, and app distribution systems may operate in more than one country, information may be processed internationally. Where relevant, we rely on provider controls, reasonable safeguards, and access restrictions appropriate to the service context.

Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights relating to access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, or consent withdrawal. We may ask for enough information to confirm your identity and the scope of your request.

Children's Privacy

We take age-sensitive usage seriously. If a product, platform rule, or applicable law requires additional safeguards, we may reduce certain data processing, limit personalization, or apply age-appropriate handling.
